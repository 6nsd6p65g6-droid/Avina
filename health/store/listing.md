**Full description** (4000):

```
Avina is your smart health companion. Measure, track and understand your body, and get clear answers to your health questions.

MEASURE WITH YOUR PHONE
• Pulse: place your fingertip on the camera and flash for 30 seconds.
• Breathing rate: rest the phone on your chest or belly for one minute.
• Hand steadiness and reaction speed.
• Vision checks: visual acuity, colour vision, Amsler grid and astigmatism.
• Hearing check with headphones, with a simple audiogram.

ASK THE AVINA DOCTOR
• Ask anything about your body, symptoms, lab tests or medicines and get a clear, careful answer.
• Answers take your profile and recent readings into account, and tell you how confident they are.
• A medical encyclopedia covering common conditions.
• Symptom checker that tells you when to see a doctor and when to call an ambulance.

TRACK YOUR HEALTH
• Log blood pressure, blood sugar, oxygen, temperature, weight and more.
• Charts, averages and a ready-to-share report for your doctor.
• Medicine reminders that arrive on time, even when the app is closed.
• Mood and anxiety check-ins.

EMERGENCY IN ONE TAP
• Your country's ambulance number, your emergency contact and your medical ID card.
• First-aid guides for stroke signs and CPR.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works without an account. Your data stays on your phone.
• Create a free account to sync securely across devices. Delete it at any time from the app.
• No ads. We never sell your data.

Available in English and Arabic.

Important: Avina is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Camera and sensor readings are estimates. Always consult a doctor, and in an emergency call your local emergency number.
```
